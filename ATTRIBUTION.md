## Sound

Every sound in the game is made in code by the audio engine (`src/audio/`) at the moment it plays: bells, taps, wind, rain, creaks, the breath, the drone and the chords. No recording is used yet. When CC0 recordings are added, each one is listed in `assets/manifest.json` with its source, author, licence and saved proof file, and it is named here.
